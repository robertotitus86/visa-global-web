# Asesoría Visa Global — Product Context

## Product Purpose
Strategic intake form: collects client information so Roberto Acosta can prepare their US visa application (DS-160) on their behalf. Clients never fill out the official government form — they answer our questions, we handle everything else.

## Users
Ecuadorian families (25–55 years old) accessing this on mobile via a WhatsApp link. Many have never filled out an online form. They are slightly anxious — this is about their dream trip or family reunification. They need to feel guided, safe, and confident that a professional is taking care of them.

## Register
brand

## Brand
- Company: Asesoría Visa Global
- Voice: Warm, professional, expert — never bureaucratic. We speak in "nosotros" (we), not Roberto personally.
- Anti-voice: cold government form, robotic instructions, legal jargon
- Core promise: "Tú solo cuéntanos — nosotros hacemos todo"

## Anti-references
- Government / embassy websites (cold, dense, bureaucratic)
- Standard form builders (JotForm, Typeform default skin)
- Cheap-looking immigration services
- Anything that looks like they're filling out paperwork

## Strategic Principles
1. One question at a time — never overwhelm
2. Explain WHY each question matters in 1 short sentence
3. Progress must feel fast and achievable
4. Mobile-first, touch-optimized
5. Warm copy at every step — this feels like talking to a trusted expert
